# Project charter / decision gates

**Mission:** discover or rule out particular physically plausible propulsion mechanisms through reproducible models and measurements. Warp-medium interaction is a supporting subproject. Novelty is not assumed.

**Nonnegotiable evidence ladder**

A. Hypothesis: explicit physical assumptions and falsifiable predictions.
B. Mathematical model: derivation and SI units checked.
C. Computational model: verification vs independent analytic benchmarks, convergence, conservation and uncertainty.
D. Hardware: safe, calibrated, blinded/control-run net-force measurement; thermal, mechanical, electric and magnetic artifacts excluded.
E. Replication: separate operators/instruments/labs.
F. Scientific review and publication: only after actual external review and consent.

**Minimum propulsion requirements**

- Define system boundary and specify where momentum goes (including remote sources, reaction mass, radiation or environmental fluid). Demonstrate integrated momentum balance; no self-force in a closed isolated system.
- Supply and measure power, mass flow, field energy or source budget; report acceleration, thrust, efficiency and waste heat with error bars.
- Show the source required to generate any proposed metric satisfies Einstein equations and examine energy conditions, horizons, control and stability; prescribed metric alone is not an actuator.
- Identify meaningful engineering limits, testable predictions and a safe prototype path.

**Evidence codes:** ESTABLISHED (textbook/paper), DERIVED-CONDITIONAL (from stated assumptions), COMPUTATION-VERIFIED (reproducible tests only), EXPERIMENTAL (measured), SPECULATIVE, UNKNOWN, REFUTED. Labels describe a *claim*, not a person or a theory's worth.

**Progress gate:** Do not announce a working mechanism until calibrated, repeatable net thrust and full conservation accounting are demonstrated and independently replicated. Unexpected results trigger extra controls before interpretation.

**Scope boundaries:** no weaponization, silo interference or replication of unverified UAP stories. UAP examples remain unverified observational hypotheses unless independently corroborated.
