# Candidate mechanisms and accounting

| Candidate | Momentum reservoir | Energy source | Simple quantitative benchmark | Open engineering issue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reaction-mass rocket | Ejected propellant | Chemical/electrical/nuclear energy | F = m_dot v_e (ideal 1D stationary rocket); jet kinetic power = 0.5 m_dot v_e^2 | Specific impulse, power, losses, propellant budget |
| Photon rocket | Directed electromagnetic radiation | Electrical/other input | F = P_radiated / c for perfectly collimated emission | Very small thrust per watt, cooling |
| Air/water propulsor | External fluid momentum | Onboard electrical/other | Momentum-flux balance, not a universal shortcut | Wake, cavitation, fluid viscosity, control |
| Solar sail | Momentum of incident/reflected sunlight | External solar radiation | Ideal normal reflecting sail F=2P_incident/c | Orientation and sunlight availability |
| Spacetime warp shell | **Not yet supplied by metric alone** | Source of curvature plus separate propulsion needed | First solve Einstein constraints and a global momentum budget | Exotic stress-energy, source engineering, controllability |
| Closed cavity / reactionless claims | No identified external momentum exchange | Electrical | No credible F under an isolated closed system without revised assumptions | Artifact rejection, external-field coupling |

These are categories for falsifiable comparison, not rankings or confirmation of novelty. All numerical benchmarks use explicitly idealized assumptions.
