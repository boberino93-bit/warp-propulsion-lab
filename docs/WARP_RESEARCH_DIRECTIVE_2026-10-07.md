# Warp Research Directive — Three-Gate Exploration Contract

**Effective date:** 2026-10-07  
**Scope:** `warp-propulsion-lab` only  
**Authorization case:** `AUTH-WARP-FORENSICS-20261007T185424Z-53A6A7`  
**Purpose:** make the project's warp-physics exploration explicit, falsifiable and non-leaky across mathematical, physical and engineering claims.

This directive does not claim a breakthrough. It changes how warp-related claims are qualified and routed.

## Mandatory gates

Every warp-related candidate is assigned to exactly one current highest-cleared gate:

- **G1 — Geometry:** Is the spacetime mathematically well-defined, nontrivial where claimed, and causally characterized?
- **G2 — Source:** Does the exact geometry admit a defensible stress-energy/source model under stated assumptions?
- **G3 — Actuation & detection:** Can the source be physically realized, controlled and measured with a preregistered observable that survives artifact controls?

A candidate may work on later-gate questions experimentally or hypothetically, but **promotion claims are sequential**. G1 does not imply G2. G2 does not imply G3.

The controlling forensic reports are:

- `docs/forensics/2026-10-07_G1_MATHEMATICAL_WARP_GEOMETRY.md`
- `docs/forensics/2026-10-07_G2_STRESS_ENERGY_ADMISSIBILITY.md`
- `docs/forensics/2026-10-07_G3_ENGINEERING_ACTUATION_DETECTION.md`

## Binding research rules

1. **Metric is not engine.** A prescribed metric, lapse, shift or shape function may establish geometry only.
2. **Source is explicit.** Any claim of physical admissibility must publish the required `T_{μν}`, observer assumptions, energy-condition analysis, conservation checks and source interpretation.
3. **Subluminal and superluminal lanes stay separate.** Evidence for one cannot be silently promoted to the other.
4. **Observer quantifiers are explicit.** A preferred observer's positive energy density is not a substitute for all-observer energy conditions.
5. **Coordinate effects are not invariant effects.** Coordinate velocity or visualization alone cannot establish physical curvature or propulsion.
6. **Force is not curvature.** A force anomaly first receives ordinary momentum/artifact analysis. Warp interpretation requires a separately predicted metric observable.
7. **Unknown systematics count against the claim.** Unbounded thermal, mechanical, EM, airflow, cable, vibration or analysis artifacts block promotion.
8. **Null results and contradictions are retained.** Failed candidates are project knowledge and may not be erased to simplify a narrative.
9. **Independent support is not vote count.** Correlated analyses, shared code/data/model lineage or common assumptions must be disclosed and grouped.
10. **UAP reports remain outside the physics input chain.** They may motivate questions but may not supply source terms, boundary conditions or validation evidence without independent calibrated data.
11. **No novelty by omission.** A missing paper, unavailable dataset or untested candidate is `UNKNOWN`, not evidence of novelty.
12. **No hardware escalation without a source/observable case.** Speculative high-energy, high-voltage, vacuum, pressure or other hazardous builds require separate safety and project authorization and cannot be justified by G1 alone.

Every new warp candidate must state:

- candidate ID and version;
- gate being tested;
- exact claim;
- assumptions;
- primary references;
- equations/model and units;
- falsifier;
- competing explanations;
- uncertainty or numerical residuals;
- provenance of code/data;
- unresolved contradictions;
- current evidence code;
- next one decisive test.

## Promotion decisions

### G1 → G2

Requires a reproducible metric definition, domain, invariant/curvature checks, causal characterization and a computed Einstein-tensor/source requirement.

### G2 → G3

Requires an assumption-matched source audit, full energy-condition disposition appropriate to the claim, conservation/resource accounting and a candidate matter/field realization or explicitly testable source surrogate.

### G3 → demonstrated mechanism

Requires calibrated end-to-end instrumentation, preregistered signal, blinded/null/reversal controls, momentum and energy closure where applicable, raw-data preservation, repeatability and independent replication.

A promotion decision must cite the evidence that closes every required item. Missing evidence blocks promotion and must be named.

## Research priorities created by this directive

1. **G1 common-formalism lane:** reproduce Alcubierre, Natário and selected generalized metrics in common 3+1 notation with invariant checks.
2. **G2 reconciliation lane:** run Bobrick-Martire / Santiago-Schuster-Visser / Fuchs-type candidates through one transparent energy-condition and source-audit framework.
3. **G3 metrology lane:** preserve the existing signed-force qualification program and add a separate curvature-observable requirements matrix.
4. **Cross-gate adversarial review:** search specifically for claim leakage, hidden observer assumptions, coordinate artifacts, missing momentum paths and unbounded systematics.
5. **Replication readiness:** package code, assumptions, raw inputs and negative results so an independent reviewer can reproduce the exact gate decision.

## Enforcement within this repository

This directive is a scientific qualification contract, not a security-authority override. Existing repository authority, mutation, scheduler, swarm, safety and provenance rules remain in force.

When this directive conflicts with a weaker scientific phrasing elsewhere in the project, the stricter three-gate interpretation governs warp-related claim promotion. It does not alter cross-project policy or grant mutation authority.

## Completion criterion

The project should not aim to "prove warp drive" as a single binary task. It should progressively answer three narrower questions:

1. **What geometries are mathematically valid?**
2. **Which of those have physically defensible sources?**
3. **Which defensible sources, if any, can be engineered and independently detected?**

A negative answer at any gate is a valid scientific result.
