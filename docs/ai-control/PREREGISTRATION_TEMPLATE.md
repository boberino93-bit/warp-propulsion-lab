# Preregistration template

Status: DRAFT until reviewed and committed before implementation.

- Question and falsifiable hypothesis:
- Evidence category and scope:
- Unit of analysis; scripted policy versus learned model:
- Baseline/control and exactly one intervention:
- Independent/dependent variables and potential confounders:
- Task-success versus permission-compliance definitions:
- Attempted versus completed unauthorized actions:
- Reward manipulation metric; independent scoring oracle:
- Detection sensitivity, false-alarm rate, time to safe exit:
- Trial count, seeds, randomization, uncertainty method:
- Frozen thresholds; exclusions; missing trials; multiple comparisons:
- Null result and falsification criteria:
- Wall-clock, event and resource budget; abort conditions:
- Network/tool prohibition; synthetic data; audit log; stop mechanism:
- Reproduction command and config/commit:
- Independent replication plan:
- Deviations log, with dates before/after seeing outcomes:

A scripted toy policy can validate the harness and controls; it cannot by itself demonstrate emergent behaviour of real models. Avoid coding the desired outcome and presenting it as discovery.
