# Literature (initial verified bibliographic pointers)

1. M. Alcubierre, *The warp drive: hyper-fast travel within general relativity*, Classical and Quantum Gravity **11**, L73-L77 (1994). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1088/0264-9381/11/5/001 . Original model requires exotic matter; metric is not an engineering device.
2. B. McMonigal, G. F. Lewis, P. O'Byrne, *Alcubierre warp drive: On the matter of matter*, Physical Review D **85**, 064024 (2012). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.85.064024 . Examines test-particle/photon paths and energy shifts, not full air/water/solid hydrodynamics.
3. A. Bobrick, G. Martire, *Introducing physical warp drives*, Classical and Quantum Gravity **38**, 105009 (2021). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6382/abdf6e . General metric classes and physical shells; explicitly concludes warp drive shells require propulsion.
