# Literature (verified bibliographic pointers)

1. M. Alcubierre, *The warp drive: hyper-fast travel within general relativity*, Classical and Quantum Gravity **11**, L73-L77 (1994). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1088/0264-9381/11/5/001 ; arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0009013 . Original model explicitly requires exotic matter; the metric is not an engineering device.
2. J. Natário, *Warp drive with zero expansion*, Classical and Quantum Gravity **19**, 1157-1165 (2002). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1088/0264-9381/19/6/308 ; arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0110086 . Demonstrates that Alcubierre's expansion/contraction narrative is not a defining feature of the entire warp-drive class.
3. B. McMonigal, G. F. Lewis, P. O'Byrne, *Alcubierre warp drive: On the matter of matter*, Physical Review D **85**, 064024 (2012). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.85.064024 . Examines test-particle/photon paths and energy shifts, not full air/water/solid hydrodynamics.
4. A. Bobrick, G. Martire, *Introducing physical warp drives*, Classical and Quantum Gravity **38**, 105009 (2021). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6382/abdf6e ; arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.06824 . Develops a general warp-spacetime framework, discusses subluminal positive-energy classes, and explicitly concludes warp shells still require propulsion.
5. J. Santiago, S. Schuster, M. Visser, *Generic warp drives violate the null energy condition*, Physical Review D **105**, 064038 (2022). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.105.064038 ; arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.03079 . Emphasizes all-observer energy-condition quantifiers and derives strong obstruction results for broad physically reasonable warp-drive classes within the framework analyzed.
6. J. Fuchs, C. Helmerich, A. Bobrick, L. Sellers, B. Melcher, G. Martire, *Constant velocity physical warp drive solution*, Classical and Quantum Gravity **41**, 095013 (2024). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6382/ad26aa ; arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.02709 . Reports a numerical constant-velocity **subluminal** warp-drive solution satisfying the standard energy conditions by combining a positive-ADM-mass matter shell with a warp-like shift distribution.
