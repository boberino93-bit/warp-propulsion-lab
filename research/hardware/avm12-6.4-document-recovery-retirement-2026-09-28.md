## Gate result

**RECOVERY FAILED; THIS AVM12-6.4 PAPER-CALCULATION CANDIDATE IS RETIRED UNDER THE FROZEN GATE.**

The public record does not authenticate the exact actuator revision used by the paper and still lacks quantitative bounds for:

- force-constant dependence on position, current and temperature;
- nonlinearity and direction-dependent hysteresis/remanence;
- temporal drift;
- covariance between actuator force constant and source current;
- a complete signed ±0.10 mN uncertainty result at both polarities.

The preregistration prohibits setting unknown terms to zero. Therefore no target current, power, combined standard uncertainty, expanded uncertainty or guard-banded performance estimate is reported.

Retirement applies only to this documentary route for this candidate. It is not evidence that voice-coil calibration is impossible, and it does not alter the paper's published 0.5–100 mN result.
