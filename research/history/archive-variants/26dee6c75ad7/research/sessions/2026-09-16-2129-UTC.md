## ONE bounded falsifiable advance
Screen an established, low-risk reaction-mass baseline against the v0.3.14 photon baseline. For a pressure-matched nozzle, NASA's generalized thrust relation reduces from `F = mdot*Ve + Ae(pe-p0)` to `F = mdot*Ve`. The expelled mass is the external momentum reservoir. Ideal exhaust kinetic power is `Pjet = 1/2 mdot Ve^2`, hence `F/Pjet = 2/Ve` when pressure thrust is zero.

Reference numerical prediction only: `mdot=1e-5 kg/s`, `Ve=100 m/s` gives `F=1e-3 N`, `Pjet=0.05 W`, and propellant use `0.6 g/min`. A 1 mN photon-emission force would require 299,792.458 W optical power; ideal externally supported reflection would require 149,896.229 W. This comparison is momentum-efficiency only; compressed-gas storage/input energy is NOT 0.05 W and was not modeled.

## Dimensions / boundaries / falsifiability
`[mdot Ve]=kg/s*m/s=N`; `[1/2 mdot Ve^2]=W`. System boundary encloses tank/valve/nozzle/balance but excludes expelled gas after it crosses the nozzle plane. Prediction is falsified if measured steady force fails to track independently measured exhaust momentum flux plus pressure thrust within uncertainty, reverses incorrectly when nozzle direction reverses, or persists when mass flow is blocked. Controls: nozzle reversal, zero-flow sham, gravimetric tank mass-loss measurement over timed runs, independent flow measurement, pressure/temperature logging, remote valve actuation, flexible-service/cable force characterization, acoustic/vibration isolation, plume impingement exclusion, ambient-airflow check, and matched thermal dummy. In vacuum, facility/plume interactions must be separately bounded.

This is established reaction propulsion, not novel propulsion. Its purpose is to validate the stand at forces far above the photon baseline and verify complete momentum accounting before unconventional candidates.
