## Bounded derivation
Use the already established Eulerian identity `(partial_t-L_beta)E + D_i j^i = K E + K_ij S^ij`, with `E=-v^2 q/(32 pi)`, `q=f_y^2+f_z^2`. Isolating only explicit terms linear in `a=dv/dt`, `L_beta E`, `D_i j^i`, and `K E` contain no explicit `a`, while `(partial_t E)_a=-v a q/(16 pi)`.

From the established ADM tensors, `K_xy=-v f_y/2`, `K_xz=-v f_z/2`, `S_xy|a=a f_y/(16 pi)`, and `S_xz|a=a f_z/(16 pi)`. Counting symmetric off-diagonal contractions twice gives `(K_ij S^ij)_a=2K_xyS_xy+2K_xzS_xz=-v a q/(16 pi)`. The acceleration-linear projection therefore closes pointwise.

For the Gaussian, `Integral q d^3x=pi^(3/2) sigma/sqrt(2)`, hence the integrated local term is `-v a sqrt(pi) sigma/(16 sqrt(2))`, exactly v0.3.11's independent chain-rule derivative of total Eulerian source energy.
