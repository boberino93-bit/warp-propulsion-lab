## Derivation
Use geometric units `c=G=1`, unit lapse, flat `gamma_ij`, and `beta_x=-v(t) f(x-x_s(t),y,z)`. Under the repository ADM convention,

`K_ij=(D_i beta_j+D_j beta_i)/2`, hence `K=-v f_x`.

For `alpha=1`, `R=0`, the ADM trace evolution equation gives

`(partial_t-L_beta)K = K_ij K^ij + 4 pi (E+S)`.

Only `partial_t K` contains the explicit acceleration `a=dv/dt`; the coefficient is `-a f_x`. Therefore

`(E+S)_acc = -a f_x/(4 pi)`.

This has the required geometric dimension `L^-2`: both `a` and `f_x` scale as `L^-1`.

## Falsifiable result
For a smooth localized shape,

`Integral (E+S)_acc d^3x = -a/(4 pi) Integral partial_x f d^3x`,

which is a boundary term and vanishes when `f` decays at infinity. A symmetric shape makes the local term odd front-to-back. Thus acceleration does require a local sign-changing source stress/energy-trace contribution, but this particular evolution-equation combination does **not** provide nonzero integrated reaction momentum/thrust.

This does not close the full problem: individual `S_ij`, energy flux, and gravitational/source momentum accounting remain to be derived. The result therefore rejects a narrower candidate mechanism, not propulsion generally.

## Negative result / error audit
No algebra/test failure occurred in this session. The important rejected leap is: local acceleration-dependent stress is **not** evidence of net thrust. Its integrated trace contribution cancels for the localized boundary conditions tested.
