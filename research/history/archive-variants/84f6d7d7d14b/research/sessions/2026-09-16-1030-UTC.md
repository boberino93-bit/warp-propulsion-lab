## Derivation and falsifiable result
For unit lapse, flat `gamma_ij`, and `beta_x=-v(t) f(x-x_s(t),y,z)`, the momentum constraint `D_j(K^{ij}-gamma^{ij}K)=8 pi S^i` uses spatial derivatives on one slice. Therefore its source is the v0.3.2 expression with instantaneous `v(t)`:

`S_x=-v(t)(f_yy+f_zz)/(16 pi)`, `S_y=v(t)f_xy/(16 pi)`, `S_z=v(t)f_xz/(16 pi)` under the declared K-sign convention.

No explicit `a=dv/dt` appears. For a smooth localized shape, all three volume integrals remain boundary terms and vanish at infinity. **Negative result:** acceleration does not expose net reaction momentum through this constraint.

This does not mean acceleration is free. `dv/dt` enters time derivatives of the geometry and hence ADM evolution equations / required spatial stresses and fluxes. Those terms are required for a closed source+field momentum budget.
