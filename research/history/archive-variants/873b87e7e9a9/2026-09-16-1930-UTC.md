## ONE TEST: SI energy and acceleration-inventory-rate scaling for the one-scale Gaussian

Prior result in geometric units G=c=1: E_geom=-k beta^2 sigma, k=sqrt(pi)/(32 sqrt(2))=0.0391660667911, beta=v_SI/c, sigma in geometric length units. Convert geometric energy length to joules by c^4/G, giving E_SI=-k(c^2/G) sigma_m v_m_s^2 = -5.27406699717748e25 [J s^2/m^3] sigma_m v_m_s^2. At fixed sigma, dE_SI/dt=-2 k(c^2/G) sigma_m v_m_s a_m_s2. This is a signed derivative of Eulerian effective source-energy inventory, NOT actuator input power or extractable energy. For v=0, instantaneous derivative is zero even at nonzero acceleration; this does not mean initiating a bubble is free.

Cases (sigma m, v m/s, a m/s^2): (1,1,1) E=-5.274066997e25 J, dE/dt=-1.054813399e26 W; (1,100,1) E=-5.274066997e29 J, rate=-1.054813399e28 W; (10,1000,10) E=-5.274066997e32 J, rate=-1.054813399e31 W; (1,0.01c,1) E=-4.740095027e38 J, rate=-3.162251017e32 W. Corresponding 1m, 1m/s signed mass equivalent E/c^2=-5.86819094e8 kg. These are formal stress-energy requirements, not usable negative mass stocks. Velocity reversal leaves E unchanged, reverses rate at fixed acceleration; deceleration changes sign. Sigma is the single Gaussian width, NOT independently tunable radius and wall thickness. Formula cannot be extrapolated to a thin-wall top-hat without reintegration.

## Dimensions, boundaries, falsifiability

[c^2/G]=kg/m; multiplied by sigma[m] v^2[m^2/s^2] yields J. Derivative adds 1/s to give W. Smooth Gaussian on all R^3, asymptotically decaying gradients, flat spatial slices, prescribed geometry and fixed sigma. No finite vessel, constitutive negative-energy source, quantum inequality check specific to this Gaussian, formation/termination cost, external momentum flux, thrust, or wall-plug power.
