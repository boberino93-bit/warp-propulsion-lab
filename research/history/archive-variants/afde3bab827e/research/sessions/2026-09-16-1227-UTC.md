## Bounded derivation
With geometric units, unit lapse, flat spatial metric and `beta_x=-v(t)f`, `K_ij=-v/2(delta_ix f_j+delta_jx f_i)`. The acceleration-only left side of the ADM K_ij evolution equation is `A_ij=-a/2(delta_ix f_j+delta_jx f_i)`. The source contribution is `-8 pi[S_ij-1/2 delta_ij(S-E)]`; Hamiltonian energy has no acceleration-only term. Taking the trace reproduces `S_acc=-a f_x/(4 pi)` from v0.3.4. Solving the tensor equation gives `Sxx_acc=0`, `Syy_acc=Szz_acc=-a f_x/(8 pi)`, `Sxy_acc=a f_y/(16 pi)`, `Sxz_acc=a f_z/(16 pi)`, `Syz_acc=0`.

The x-directed coordinate stress divergence is `d_j S_xj = a(f_yy+f_zz)/(16 pi)`. For a smooth localized shape its all-space integral is a boundary term and vanishes. A deliberately nonlocalized test profile retains force, correctly exposing boundary momentum exchange rather than isolated reactionless thrust.

**Negative propulsion result:** the full acceleration-only spatial stress tensor does not expose a surviving integrated longitudinal reaction force. The exact `Sxx_acc=0` cancellation is stronger than the previous trace result. Local shear/transverse stresses remain required, so acceleration is not source-free.
