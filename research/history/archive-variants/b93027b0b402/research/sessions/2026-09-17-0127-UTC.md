## ONE bounded advance
Define a conservative numerical eligibility gate before any unconventional candidate can be tested. Random null-channel terms combine in quadrature, `sigma_art=sqrt(sum sigma_i^2)`. Unknown-sign systematic artifact bounds (thermal, electromagnetic, cable, airflow, etc.) add absolutely, `B_art=sum B_i`; cancellation is forbidden. Minimum detectable thrust is predeclared as `F_MDT=B_art+5 sigma_art`. Artifact-corrected SNR is `max(0, |F_meas|-B_art)/sigma_art`. A candidate is instrument-eligible only if `F_MDT` is below the weakest independently calibrated reference (currently 0.1 mN) AND `|F_meas| >= F_MDT`. Passing is not evidence of new propulsion; it only establishes that the stand could discriminate a force at that scale.

Illustrative falsifiable case (not measured data): independent null sigmas 3 and 4 microN give `sigma_art=5 microN`; systematic bounds totaling 10 microN give `F_MDT=35 microN`. A 35 microN signal is exactly corrected SNR=5 and the floor is below the 0.1 mN calibration reference; 34 microN fails. If random sigma rises to 20 microN with 10 microN systematic bound, `F_MDT=110 microN`, so the stand is ineligible even for a larger apparent signal until artifacts are reduced.

## Controls / literature
Thermal null: matched-power no-thrust/sham and time-lag correlation. EM null: actuator/thruster power applied in a momentum-neutral configuration plus polarity/reversal. Cable null: identical current/power routing with force source disabled; NASA explicitly reports wire Joule-heating deformation causing time-varying force/torque and used liquid-gallium feeds to remove it (Polzin et al., NASA NTRS 20060025538). Airflow null: enclosed/no-flow sham and orientation reversal; vacuum is a later escalation, not assumed here. Randomized order and before/during/after in-situ calibration are required. Markusic et al. (AIAA 2004-3441, NTRS 20040085923) used active thermal control and in-situ calibration to counter drift. Haag (NASA-TM-107066, 1995) reported ~1% zero drift during repetitive PPT testing; Haag (AIAA 89-2829) reduced applied-field magnetic tares below 3% and measured them independently. These are precedents for measuring/subtracting tares, not universal artifact values.

## Evidence status / falsification
No hardware was built and no thrust was measured. The numerical example is synthetic. Falsify eligibility if any null-channel systematic bound is unquantified, if `F_MDT >= 0.1 mN`, if sign/reversal behavior is inconsistent, or if the apparent candidate force is below `F_MDT`. An unexplained null failure is a negative result and blocks candidate testing.
