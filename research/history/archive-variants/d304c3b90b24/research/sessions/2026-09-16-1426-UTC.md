## Bounded derivation
Repository convention has `j_x=-v(f_yy+f_zz)/(16 pi)`. For rigid translation `f=f(x-x_s(t),y,z)`, `partial_t f=-v f_x`. Hence at fixed coordinates

`partial_t j_x = -a(f_yy+f_zz)/(16 pi) + v^2 partial_x(f_yy+f_zz)/(16 pi)`.

v0.3.6 already established pointwise cancellation of the first term by the acceleration stress divergence. If the flat-looking partial equation used there were incorrectly promoted to the full nonlinear law, the remainder would be

`R_naive = v^2 partial_x(f_yy+f_zz)/(16 pi)`.

For `f=exp(-r^2/sigma^2)` on the x-axis, `f_yy+f_zz=-4f/sigma^2` and `partial_x(f_yy+f_zz)=8 x f/sigma^4`. Therefore the residual is locally nonzero away from the center, is odd in x, even in v, and integrates to zero for a localized symmetric profile.
