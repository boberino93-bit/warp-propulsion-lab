## Bounded derivation
v0.3.9 gives `E_total=-v^2 sqrt(pi) sigma/(32 sqrt(2))`. For fixed Gaussian scale and `a=dv/dt`,

`P_acc = dE_total/dt = -v a sqrt(pi) sigma/(16 sqrt(2))`.

Thus acceleration in the direction of current velocity makes the required negative Eulerian energy inventory more negative; braking makes it less negative. At `v=0`, the instantaneous first derivative is zero even for nonzero acceleration because the inventory is quadratic in v. This is an inventory derivative, not demonstrated actuator input power.

## Dimensions / boundary / source audit
In G=c=1, integrated energy has dimension L; v is dimensionless, a has L^-1, sigma has L, so `P_acc` is dimensionless as required for dL/dt. Assumes smooth Gaussian decay at infinity and fixed sigma. No finite boundary, constitutive stress-energy generator, reaction reservoir or control mechanism is supplied.
