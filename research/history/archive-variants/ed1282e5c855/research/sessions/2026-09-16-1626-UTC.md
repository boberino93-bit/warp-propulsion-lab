## Bounded derivation
Hamiltonian constraint result already established in the merged tree:

`E = -v^2[(f_y)^2+(f_z)^2]/(32 pi)`.

For `f=exp(-r^2/sigma^2)`, `f_y=-2yf/sigma^2`, `f_z=-2zf/sigma^2`. Gaussian integration over `R^3` gives

`Integral[(f_y)^2+(f_z)^2] d^3x = pi^(3/2) sigma/sqrt(2)`

and therefore

`E_total = -v^2 sqrt(pi) sigma/(32 sqrt(2))`.

The result is nonpositive, invariant under `v -> -v`, quadratic in speed, and linear in the single Gaussian scale. Local `E` has geometric dimension `L^-2`; the volume integral has dimension `L`. SI energy is obtained by multiplying the geometric length by `c^4/G`.

## Boundary / source audit
The analytic integral assumes a smooth Gaussian decaying at spatial infinity. There is no finite boundary supplying momentum or energy. The result is a requirement inferred from Einstein's constraint for a prescribed geometry; no constitutive matter/field model capable of generating it has been demonstrated.
