## Derivation
For `ds^2=-dt^2+(dx-v f dt)^2+dy^2+dz^2`, `alpha=1`, `gamma_ij=delta_ij`, `beta_x=-v f`, and convention `K_ij=(D_i beta_j+D_j beta_i)/2`:

- `K_xx=-v f_x`, `K_xy=-v f_y/2`, `K_xz=-v f_z/2`, `K=-v f_x`.
- Applying `D_j(K^{ij}-gamma^{ij}K)=8 pi S^i` gives
  - `S_x=-v(f_yy+f_zz)/(16 pi)`
  - `S_y= v f_xy/(16 pi)`
  - `S_z= v f_xz/(16 pi)`.

The opposite extrinsic-curvature sign convention flips all three signs. In either convention, source momentum is locally nonzero and linear in `v`.

For a smooth localized shape whose derivatives vanish at infinity, volume integrals of all three components are boundary terms and vanish. Therefore the momentum constraint does **not** expose a net reaction momentum that could propel an isolated craft. It specifies local momentum that the still-unknown geometry-generating source must carry.
