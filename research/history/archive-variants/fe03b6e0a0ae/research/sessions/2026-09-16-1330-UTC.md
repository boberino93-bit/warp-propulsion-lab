## Bounded derivation
Repository convention gives the momentum-constraint source density

`j_x = -v (f_yy+f_zz)/(16 pi)`.

At fixed spatial coordinates, the explicit velocity derivative contributes

`(partial_t j_x)_a = -a (f_yy+f_zz)/(16 pi)`.

The v0.3.5 acceleration stress has `Sxx=0`, `Sxy=a f_y/(16 pi)`, `Sxz=a f_z/(16 pi)`, so

`(partial_j S_xj)_a = +a (f_yy+f_zz)/(16 pi)`.

Therefore

`(partial_t j_x)_a + (partial_j S_xj)_a = 0`

**pointwise**, not merely after volume integration. The acceleration-dependent shear stress is exactly the local momentum-flux channel required to change the prescribed source momentum density at this order.

## Propulsion result
**Negative.** No external reaction channel or surviving net thrust appears at acceleration order. Instead, local source momentum change is balanced internally by divergence of the required stress. This strengthens v0.3.5's global boundary cancellation.

This does not prove full conservation for a realizable drive. Terms from translation of the shape and nonlinear shift/advection scale as `v^2` and were deliberately excluded. A physical constitutive model for the required stress-energy is still absent.
