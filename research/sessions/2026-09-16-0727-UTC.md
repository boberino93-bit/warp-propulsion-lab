## 2. Scope and derivation

For **prescribed 1+1 dimensional shift** `ds²=-dt²+(dx-v_s*f(x-v_s*t)dt)²` in `c=1` geometric units, choose Eulerian dust with `dx/dt=v_s*f` and rest density `rho` (not a general velocity field). Let `xi=x-v_s*t` and `xi0=xi(0)`. Under no pressure, no transverse motion, constant `v_s`, no backreaction and initially uniform Eulerian density:

```
partial_t rho + partial_x(rho v_s f) = 0
xi_dot = v_s(f(xi)-1)
log(rho)_dot = -v_s f'(xi)
J = partial xi(t;xi0)/partial xi0
log(J)_dot = v_s f'(xi), J(0)=1
rho(t)=rho_initial/J(t)
```

Independently, along trajectories:

```
rho(t) * [1 - f(xi(t))] = rho_initial(xi0) * [1 - f(xi0)]
rho(t) = rho_initial(xi0) * [1-f(xi0)] / [1-f(xi(t))], when f<1
```

Consequently, inserting the *steady* `rho0/(1-f)` for an **initially uniform** medium is wrong except in suitable far-upstream limits. `f=1` cannot support a finite steady, nonzero-throughput density in the old expression. A 1+1 restriction of a metric is not a self-consistent 3+1 Einstein-matter spacetime.
