## Question and new bounded result
Do the stated sonar distance and duration determine the claimed peak speed?
No. They determine a relative average speed, subject to interpreting the stated separation and duration correctly. This is a demonstrable inverse-problem ambiguity, not evidence that the event was fabricated.

Distance D=2500 m, duration T=25 s: D/T=100 m/s=223.693629 mph.
A rest-to-rest triangular speed profile lasting the full interval has peak 2D/T=200 m/s and acceleration 4D/T^2=16 m/s^2.
A triangular pulse lasting tau<=T with zero speed outside it travels the same D when peak=2D/tau. Setting peak=3000 mph=1341.12 m/s gives tau=3.728227 s. Thus both much lower and claimed peak speeds fit the endpoint facts with different histories. These are kinematic counterexamples, not physically viable submarine models.
With no acceleration/speed bound, the endpoint data alone supply no finite upper bound on peak speed. Continuous tracks, Doppler, ship motion, target association and timing are needed.

## Conditional descent calculation
For rest-to-rest displacement D and duration T with |a|<=A, v(t)<=A*min(t,T-t), so D<=A*T^2/4. Equality is attained by symmetric constant acceleration/braking. Therefore A>=4D/T^2 under those endpoint conditions.
At D=28000*0.3048=8534.4 m and T=.78 s, bound=5721.673930 standard g; equality-profile peak=21883.076923 m/s.
This uses spoken rounded numbers, not the paper's complete statistical model; it is not a correction of its reported 5370 g estimate. Unknown endpoint velocities change the bound. A proportional timing change matters quadratically: doubling T quarters the bound.
Mass is not needed for kinematic acceleration. Kinetic energy requires mass and frame; power requires a force/velocity history. Missing observed heat is not proof of zero energy transfer, nor does ordinary kinetic-energy accounting establish a warped-spacetime budget.

## Why aggregation does not yet identify origin
For hypotheses N (nonhuman technology) and A (specified alternatives), posterior odds equal prior odds times P(E1,...,En|N)/P(E1,...,En|A).
The likelihood factorizes into conditional terms P(Ei|H,E1,...,Ei-1), not automatically independent P(Ei|H). A verbatim retelling of the same datum adds no independent likelihood factor once the datum is known.
Neither likelihood models nor source dependence nor a complete set of alternatives are established here. Therefore no defensible numerical odds for aliens follow from counting examples.
Likewise, a dataset describing motion alone generally cannot distinguish human-built and nonhuman-built devices if both hypotheses are allowed the same unspecified mechanism. Builder provenance needs further evidence. This is not a claim all reports are mundane or equally plausible.

## Medium and propulsion implications
Sonar scattering and absence of a noticed surface wake are different observables. Acoustic coupling can exist without an observer noticing a surface disturbance; quantitative compatibility needs geometry, depth, frequency and sensitivity. Do not treat either as zero coupling.
Water has finite compressibility and can flow laterally; forced lifting of an entire cylinder is not a unique displacement model. No real-fluid or solid solver was added.
For propulsion, choose a boundary including craft/source and account for momentum transferred to exhaust, radiation, fields and medium. No force or power can be measured from this interview. A prescribed metric and coordinate motion are not a demonstrated actuator.
