## ONE bounded advance: Hamiltonian constraint for standard 3+1 metric
Assume the prescribed standard Alcubierre metric in geometric units G=c=1: `ds²=-dt²+(dx-v f(r)dt)²+dy²+dz²`, `r²=(x-x_s(t))²+y²+z²`; lapse 1, flat spatial metric gamma_ij=delta_ij, shift beta^x=-v f. Adopt `K_ij=(D_i beta_j+D_j beta_i-partial_t gamma_ij)/2` (opposite overall sign convention yields same Hamiltonian energy). The only nonzero components are `K_xx=-v f_x`, `K_xy=-v f_y/2`, `K_xz=-v f_z/2`. Thus `K²=v² f_x²`, `K_ij K^ij=v²[f_x²+(f_y²+f_z²)/2]` and `3R+K²-K_ij K^ij= -v²(f_y²+f_z²)/2`. Einstein Hamiltonian constraint `16 pi E=3R+K²-KijKij` gives **Eulerian source energy density** `E=-v²(f_y²+f_z²)/(32 pi) <=0`. For radial f, `E=-v² (f'(r))² (y²+z²)/(32 pi r²)`. This is a *required* source projection for the prescribed metric, not a construction or energy-generation mechanism. A longitudinal-only gradient cancels; nonzero transverse wall gradients imply negative Eulerian energy, including at subluminal nonzero v. At fixed shape, magnitude scales v²; at fixed dimensionless profile f(r/R), local E scales v²/R². Thin-wall integrated-energy scaling is not numerically established in this session.

SI units: energy density [J/m³] = `-c² v_s²/(32 pi G) * (f_y²+f_z²)` with v_s in m/s and gradients in 1/m; equivalently `-(c⁴/(32 pi G))*(v_s/c)²*(f_y²+f_z²)`. This follows from geometric-to-SI stress energy factor c⁴/G and v=v_s/c. Not a mass density unless divided by c².
