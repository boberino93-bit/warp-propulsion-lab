## Derivation and result
In geometric units `c=G=1`, unit lapse, flat spatial metric, `beta_x=-v(t)f`, and the repository ADM convention, `K=-v f_x`. The trace evolution equation `(partial_t-L_beta)K=K_ij K^ij+4 pi(E+S)` gives the acceleration-only contribution `(E+S)_acc=-a f_x/(4 pi)`. Both `a` and `f_x` scale as `L^-1`, giving the required `L^-2` source dimension.

For smooth localized `f`, its volume integral is `-a/(4 pi) Integral partial_x f d^3x`, a boundary term that vanishes at infinity. A symmetric bubble makes the local term odd front-to-back. **Negative result:** acceleration requires local sign-changing stress/energy-trace structure, but this combination supplies no nonzero integrated reaction term by itself. Individual stresses, energy flux and full gravitational/source momentum accounting remain open.
