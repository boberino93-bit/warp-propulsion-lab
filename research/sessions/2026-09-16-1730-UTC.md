## Bounded derivation
For `ds^2=-dt^2+(dx-v f dt)^2+dy^2+dz^2`, `alpha=1`, flat spatial metric, `beta^x=-v f`, and rigid `f=F(x-vt,y,z)`, the repository Eulerian projection is `(partial_t-L_beta)E + D_i j^i = K E + K_ij S^ij`.

A fresh independent SymPy construction of `g`, `g^-1`, all Christoffels, Ricci tensor/scalar and Einstein tensor reproduced `E=-v^2(f_y^2+f_z^2)/(32 pi)` and repository momentum density. It gives `D_i j^i=0` identically. Direct contraction with the Einstein-derived spatial stress gives both sides `-v^3 (f_y f_xy+f_z f_xz)(f-1)/(16 pi)`. Therefore the Eulerian energy equation closes pointwise. For a Gaussian, `f_y f_xy+f_z f_xz=-8x(y^2+z^2)f^2/sigma^6`, so the local work/redistribution is odd front-to-back and its all-space integral is zero. Rigid translation also leaves the already-established total Eulerian energy constant. No net constant-speed power demand is inferred from this global derivative, despite nonzero local redistribution and a nonzero negative source-energy inventory.
