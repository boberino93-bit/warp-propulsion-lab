## ONE bounded falsifiable advance
Define a physically independent non-contact magnetic force-injection cross-calibration channel. Model the characterized actuator over its validated linear range as `F_mag = k I`, with reversal under current reversal. The momentum boundary is explicit: this is NOT self-contained propulsion; equal/opposite reaction momentum is carried by the stationary magnetic actuator/support/environment outside the thrust-stand pendulum boundary.

For independent 1-sigma current and force-constant uncertainties, `sigma_F^2=(k sigma_I)^2+(I sigma_k)^2`. Cross-calibration against the deadweight channel uses `z=(F_mag-F_dw)/sqrt(sigma_mag^2+sigma_dw^2+sigma_repeat^2)` and requires same sign, `|z|<=3`, and <=5% fractional difference. This is a predeclared engineering gate, not evidence of anomalous thrust.

Illustrative scaling only: if `k=0.01 N/A`, 10–100 mA spans 0.1–1.0 mN. This is not a hardware prescription and does not assert that any particular coil/magnet geometry has that constant. `k` and its uncertainty must be independently characterized for the actual apparatus; field/geometry, alignment, heating, magnetic coupling to the stand, current measurement, hysteresis and position dependence remain experimental systematic terms.

## Primary/authoritative evidence
NASA Wong et al. (2011), *Non-Contact Thrust Stand Calibration Method for Repetitively-Pulsed Electric Thrusters*, NTRS 20110015846: a solenoidal coil acts on a permanent magnet mounted to a pendulum arm; reported calibration coefficient error was roughly 1%, and the method explicitly treats pulse-period/stand-dynamics coupling. https://ntrs.nasa.gov/archive/nasa/casi.ntrs.nasa.gov/20110015846.pdf

NIST Kibble Dynamic Force Reference: electromagnetic force can be made traceable through synchronous electrical/velocity measurements; NIST emphasizes impedance corrections and dynamic-system effects, with targeted uncertainty around 1%. https://www.nist.gov/noac/technology/mass-force-and-acceleration/kibble-dynamic-force-reference

NIST traceability policy: traceability belongs to a measurement result through a documented unbroken calibration chain; merely using nominally calibrated equipment is insufficient. https://www.nist.gov/metrology/metrological-traceability

## Controls / falsification
Required before use: zero-current sham; +/− current reversal; randomized levels spanning the calibrated range; independent current measurement; position sweep to bound force-gradient sensitivity; thermal soak/sham to detect coil heating drift; magnetic blank/shield geometry control where appropriate; cable-routing control; airflow/vibration monitoring; deadweight calibration before/after; repeated cross-channel trials. Reject the channel if sign fails, any level has `|z|>3`, >5% channel disagreement, nonlinearity/hysteresis exceeds the declared uncertainty, or unexplained thermal/magnetic coupling remains comparable to the target force.
