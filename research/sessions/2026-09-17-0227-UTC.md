## ONE bounded advance
Predeclared a balanced blinded campaign with ON+, ON-, OFF and SHAM conditions and a frozen analysis threshold before labels are revealed. Added a deterministic randomized schedule generator and Monte Carlo injection model. For the v0.3.19 illustrative artifact floor (`sigma_art=5 µN`, systematic bound `B=10 µN`), 16 samples per polarity give campaign mean random uncertainty `sigma_mean = sigma_art/sqrt(32) = 0.8839 µN`; the deliberately conservative frozen threshold is `B + 5 sigma_mean = 14.4194 µN`.

Monte Carlo, 20,000 synthetic campaigns per point, fixed seed 319: 0 µN injection: detection 0, null false-positive 0; 10 µN: detection 0; 14.4194 µN: detection 0.49685; 15 µN: 0.74315; 20 µN: 1.0; 35 µN: 1.0. Zero observed false positives in 20,000 simulations is NOT proof of zero false-positive probability; it only bounds what was observed under this Gaussian/common-mode synthetic model. A common non-reversing bias cancels under polarity sign alignment, but unknown reversal-correlated artifacts remain a falsification risk and must be measured experimentally.

## Controls / falsification
Run order is randomized and balanced. Operator-facing run IDs must be opaque; condition key remains withheld until preprocessing, exclusions, threshold and analysis code are frozen. OFF and matched-power SHAM estimate non-thrust backgrounds; ON polarity reversal tests sign behavior. Thermal, EM, cable and airflow controls from v0.3.19 remain mandatory. Any label-dependent exclusion, threshold retuning after unblinding, reversal-correlated artifact, calibration drift beyond uncertainty, or false-positive rate above the preregistered allowance invalidates the campaign.

NASA precedent supports in-situ thrust calibration and explicit thermal/wiring-drift control (Polzin et al., NTRS 20060025538), non-contact magnetic calibration with ~1% coefficient error (Wong et al., NTRS 20110015846), and independent uncertainty propagation for thrust stands (Mackey et al., NASA/TM-2018-219952). These precedents do not validate this synthetic campaign or any unconventional propulsion claim.
