## ONE bounded advance
Stress-tested the frozen v0.3.20 polarity-aligned threshold against a stationary AR(1) time-correlated noise model plus a reversal-correlated zero-thrust artifact. The artifact is deliberately defined as `+b` in ON+ and `-b` in ON-, so the estimator `(mean(ON+) - mean(ON-))/2` returns `b`: this is mathematically indistinguishable from a true polarity-reversing force unless an independent control measures or breaks the coupling.

Frozen illustrative parameters remain `sigma_art=5 µN`, `B=10 µN`, `n=16` per polarity, `z=5`, threshold `14.4194 µN`. Fixed-seed 20,000-trial synthetic null campaigns with reversal artifact `b=10 µN` gave false-positive rates: rho=0: 0; rho=0.5: 0.00105; rho=0.9: 0.05135; rho=0.99: 0.09455. With independent noise (rho=0), `b=15 µN` produced 0.74375 false positives and `b=20 µN` produced 1.0. These are model outputs, not empirical instrument rates.

## Interpretation / correction
The previous Gaussian/common-mode result was too narrow to support the frozen threshold as a general false-positive gate. A common non-reversing bias cancels, but reversal-correlated bias does not. Strong temporal correlation also reduces the effective averaging benefit: at the nominal 10 µN artifact bound, rho=0.9 already produced about a 5% synthetic false-positive rate. Therefore the current threshold is **rejected as hardware-ready** until the actual stand's autocorrelation and reversal-correlated response are experimentally bounded.

## Controls / falsification
Before any unconventional test, characterize autocorrelation from long zero-load records; perform polarity-command sham runs with the candidate physically unable to generate thrust; randomize/interleave polarity rather than using long polarity blocks; independently monitor thermal, magnetic, electrical, cable-force and airflow variables; and require the measured reversal-correlated artifact bound plus correlated-noise uncertainty to be below the preregistered detection threshold. If sham reversal reproduces the candidate signal or correlated-null false positives exceed the preregistered allowance, reject the propulsion claim/protocol.
