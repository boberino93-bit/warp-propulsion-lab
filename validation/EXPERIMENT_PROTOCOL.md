# Preliminary falsification and artifact rejection plan

No experimental propulsion design exists yet. Before a proposed prototype test:

1. Fix the entire system boundary: include cables, power feeds, supports, nearby conductive and magnetic masses, airflow and emitted radiation.
2. Specify expected thrust as a function of power and geometry plus conservation-accounted source/sink.
3. Calibrate thrust balance using known loads; quantify drift, vibration, heating/thermal convection, electrostatic forces, magnetic field coupling and acoustic effects.
4. Pre-register blinded polarity/orientation reversals, dummy loads, power-off and shielded controls, and signal-processing methods.
5. Report raw data, error bars, negative results, pre/post calibrations and independent replication. No conclusion from apparent force alone.

**No build instructions for high-voltage, high-power or hazardous hardware in this initial scaffold.**
